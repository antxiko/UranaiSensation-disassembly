It is not a game: it is a fortune-teller. Up to twenty people are entered with their name, their date, time and place of birth, their sex and their blood type, and a penguin tells them five fortunes: the match of two people by blood type, a temple omikuji, the horoscope, palm reading and the list of people. The first Konami disk in the series: its own boot, a kernel and 41 parts loaded by sectors. The screens come from running the disk; the main one, checked against openMSX: 0 bytes different in the 128 KB of VRAM.

Konami - (c) 1988 - 720 KB disk (its own boot, no MSX-DOS) - MSX2 - Dump f9499b97...

There are no screenshots here. Every screen comes from running the disk on a Z80 and a V9938 written for this (tools/uranai_run.py), pressing the keys its code asks for, and our own code paints them from the VRAM.

What is inside

The program

  • The fortunes: the five fortunes, screen by screen, and how each result comes out of the code.
  • The texts: the texts of the screens, with their translation.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: leftovers of the development disk, an old kernel, the handy printer and what is still not known.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the disk, the map of its 1,440 sectors, the boot, the kernel, the parts and the tools.
